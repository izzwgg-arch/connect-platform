Everything included

A complete phone system,
not a bag of add-ons

Every Connect plan ships with the whole feature set. No nickel-and-diming, no “Enterprise-only” routing.

Simultaneous ring

Desk phone, desktop app and mobile ring together. Answer anywhere; the others stop instantly. Move a live call between devices without dropping it.

Visual voicemail

See, play and manage voicemails like email — with voicemail-to-email delivery and greetings you record by answering one call.

IVR Studio

Build call menus visually — “Press 1 for orders, 2 for the office” — with business-hours, holiday and override schedules.

Ring groups & queues

Ring teams together or in order. Queue busy periods with hold music, announcements, and live agent controls.

Call recording

Record calls with per-company controls, searchable playback in the portal, and automatic transcription — in English and Yiddish.

Business SMS & WhatsApp

Text from your business number — shared team inboxes, campaigns, and a WhatsApp inbox alongside your calls.

Live wallboards & reports

Watch calls in real time, then dig into history: call reports, queue performance, call-quality diagnostics and a full call timeline for every conversation. When something sounds off, we can tell you exactly why.

Team chat & presence

Internal chat with attachments and voice notes, plus live presence — see who’s available, busy or on a call before you transfer.

Apps for every screen

Native apps that treat calls like calls

Connect’s mobile apps plug into the real phone experience on iPhone and Android — calls ring on your lock screen like any other call, survive the app being closed, and answer instantly. The Windows desktop app and browser phone keep your desk covered.

  • ✓iPhone & Android — native incoming-call screens, Bluetooth and CarPlay-friendly audio, background reliability.
  • ✓Windows desktop — full softphone with a mini-dialer that stays out of your way.
  • ✓Browser phone — take calls from the portal with zero installs.
  • ✓Desk phones — we provision and manage supported desk phones for you.
  • ✓Contacts that follow you — company directory plus your device contacts, synced both ways.
