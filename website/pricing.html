Pricing FAQ

The fine print, in plain English

Are there setup fees?
Standard onboarding is included — our automated setup builds your numbers, extensions and apps at no charge. Complex migrations (many locations, desk-phone fleets) are quoted upfront.
What about taxes and fees?
Like every phone provider, we collect applicable telecom taxes and regulatory fees, including the E911 fee. They're itemized on every invoice — no surprises, no padding.
Can I bring my own numbers?
Yes, porting is free and we manage the process. Your existing service keeps working until the moment the port completes.
Is there a contract?
Plans are month-to-month. Annual billing is available at a discount if you prefer it.
How is Connect Plus priced?
Delivery operations pricing depends on stores, drivers and order volume — we'll quote it in one call, and you'll know exactly what it costs before anything starts.
