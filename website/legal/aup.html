Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: [Month DD, YYYY]

This Acceptable Use Policy (“AUP”) applies to all use of Loop’s Services. It exists to protect our customers, the public telephone network, and the carriers we interconnect with. Violations may result in suspension or termination under the Terms of Service.

1. Lawful use only

You may use the Services only in compliance with all applicable laws and regulations, including the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA), the Telemarketing Sales Rule, the TRACED Act, the CAN-SPAM Act, CTIA messaging guidelines, and state telemarketing and call-recording laws.

2. Prohibited voice activity

  • Illegal robocalling or auto-dialed calls without required consent, and any prerecorded telemarketing without prior express written consent.
  • Caller ID spoofing with intent to defraud, cause harm, or wrongfully obtain anything of value. You may only display caller IDs for numbers you are authorized to use.
  • Calls to numbers on the National Do Not Call Registry in violation of law.
  • Traffic pumping, access stimulation, toll fraud, or artificially inflating traffic.
  • Harassing, threatening, or fraudulent calls of any kind, including one-ring (Wangiri) schemes.
  • Reselling the Services or using them as a wholesale trunking substitute without a written agreement.

3. Prohibited messaging activity

  • Sending SMS/MMS without the recipient consent required by law and by our SMS & Messaging Policy.
  • Ignoring opt-outs: STOP requests must always be honored immediately.
  • Content prohibited by carrier rules (SHAFT: sex, hate, alcohol without age-gating, firearms, tobacco; plus illegal content of any kind, phishing, and deceptive links).
  • Snowshoeing, number cycling, or other techniques to evade carrier spam filtering.

4. Network integrity and security

  • No attempts to probe, breach, or disrupt Loop's systems or any other network.
  • No credential sharing that defeats per-user identity, and no unauthorized access to other tenants' data.
  • No use of the platform to distribute malware or operate command-and-control infrastructure.
  • We may apply reasonable rate limits and fraud controls (for example, international-calling limits) to protect you and the network.

5. Recording consent

Call recording laws vary by state — some require the consent of every participant. You are responsible for enabling recording only where lawful and for playing any required notifications. Recording features are provided as tools; the legal obligation is yours.

6. AI features

You may not use AI features to generate unlawful content, to impersonate others with intent to deceive, or to attempt to bypass the platform’s permission and approval systems.

7. Reporting abuse

To report abuse of our network (unwanted calls or texts appearing to originate from our Services), contact abuse@YOURDOMAIN.com. We investigate every report and cooperate with carriers, the Industry Traceback Group, and law enforcement as required.

8. Enforcement

We may throttle, suspend, or terminate service for AUP violations — immediately and without notice where required to protect the network or comply with carrier or regulatory demands, and with notice otherwise. We may report unlawful activity to authorities.
