Privacy Policy

Last updated: [Month DD, YYYY] · Effective: [Month DD, YYYY]

This Privacy Policy describes how Loop Communications LLC (“Loop,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our websites, our cloud communications service (Connect), our AI agent services, our delivery-operations software (Connect Plus), our contractor software (Loop Field), and our mobile and desktop applications (together, the “Services”).

Plain-English summary: We collect what we need to run your phone system and software, we don’t sell your personal information, and your business’s data belongs to your business. Call recordings and messages are your content — we store and process them only to provide the Services you configured.

1. Information we collect

Account and contact information

Name, business name, email address, phone number, billing address, and account credentials when you sign up or contact us.

Communications data

Because we provide a phone service, we process call detail records (numbers, times, durations, routing), voicemails, text messages sent and received through the Services, call recordings (where enabled by your account administrator), and transcriptions. This content is processed to deliver the Services and is available to your account’s authorized users according to permissions your administrator sets.

Usage and device information

App version, device type, operating system, IP address, log data, and diagnostic events that help us keep calls connecting and apps working.

Location information

For delivery-tracking features, driver location is collected from the driver app while a delivery shift is active, as configured by the employer account. For E911 service, we maintain the registered service address you provide.

Payment information

Payments are processed by our payment partners. Loop never stores your full card number or CVV.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain and improve the Services — connecting calls, delivering messages, routing deliveries, generating invoices.
  • To provide AI features you use — for example transcribing a voicemail, answering a support chat, or executing a change you request. AI actions on your account are logged and auditable.
  • To monitor service health, prevent fraud and abuse, and secure the platform.
  • To bill you and comply with tax and telecom regulatory obligations.
  • To communicate with you about the Services, including service notifications and support.

3. How we share information

  • Carriers and interconnection partners — to route your calls and messages to and from the public telephone network, as any phone company must.
  • Service providers — hosting, payment processing, transcription and AI model providers, under contracts limiting their use of your data to providing services to us.
  • Emergency services — your registered address and callback number are transmitted to emergency responders when you dial 911.
  • Legal compliance — when required by law, subpoena, or to protect rights and safety.
  • Business transfers — as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, with notice to you.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use the content of your calls, messages or recordings to train generalized AI models.

4. CPNI (Customer Proprietary Network Information)

Certain data about your use of telecommunications services (such as call records) is protected as CPNI under FCC rules. We use CPNI to provide the Services and as permitted by law. You may restrict our use of CPNI for marketing by contacting us; doing so will not affect your service.

5. Data retention

We retain account data for as long as your account is active and as required for legal, billing and tax purposes. Communications content (voicemails, recordings, messages) is retained according to your plan’s storage settings and your administrator’s configuration, and can be deleted by authorized users. Call detail records are retained as required by regulation.

6. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls, tenant isolation (your business’s data is segregated from other customers), audit logging, and continuous monitoring. No system is perfectly secure; we notify affected customers of breaches as required by law.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access, correct or delete your account information from the portal or by contacting us.
  • Opt out of marketing emails via the unsubscribe link; transactional service messages will still be sent.
  • Depending on your state or country (e.g., California, the EU/UK), you may have additional rights to access, delete, correct, or port your personal information, and to not be discriminated against for exercising them. Contact us at privacy@YOURDOMAIN.com to exercise these rights.

8. Children

The Services are business tools and are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes here and update the date above. Material changes will be notified to account owners by email or in-app notice.
