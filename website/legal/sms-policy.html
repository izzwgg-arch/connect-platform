SMS & Messaging Policy

The rules for sending text messages through Loop — consent, opt-outs, and content.

Last updated: [Month DD, YYYY]

This policy applies to all SMS/MMS and messaging features of the Services, including one-to-one texting, shared inboxes, automated notifications (such as delivery updates), and campaigns. It supplements the Acceptable Use Policy and reflects carrier A2P/10DLC requirements and CTIA guidelines.

1. Registration

U.S. carriers require business messaging to be registered (10DLC brand and campaign registration). Loop manages this registration with you at onboarding. Sending campaigns from unregistered numbers is not permitted and will be filtered by carriers.

2. Consent (opt-in)

  • Conversational messaging: if a customer texts your business first, you may reply within that conversation.
  • Informational messaging (order updates, delivery notifications, appointment reminders): requires the recipient's prior consent — for example, providing their number for that purpose at order time.
  • Promotional messaging (marketing, offers, campaigns): requires prior express written consent, with clear disclosure of message frequency and “message and data rates may apply.”
  • Keep records of consent. You must be able to show when and how each recipient opted in.

3. Opt-out

  • Every recipient can opt out at any time by replying STOP (and similar keywords). Opt-outs are honored automatically by the platform and must never be overridden.
  • Promotional campaigns must include opt-out language (e.g., “Reply STOP to unsubscribe”) at least in the first message and periodically thereafter.
  • HELP replies return your business identification and contact information.

4. Sender identification

Messages must identify your business. Do not send messages that disguise or misrepresent the sender.

5. Prohibited content

  • Content that is illegal, deceptive, or infringes others' rights.
  • Carrier-prohibited categories (SHAFT): sexually explicit content, hate speech, alcohol without age verification, firearms, tobacco/vape.
  • High-risk financial services, debt collection, or lead-generation content prohibited by carrier programs.
  • Phishing, malware links, or link shorteners that obscure the destination domain.

6. Sending practices

  • Respect quiet hours customary in your recipients' time zones (generally 8 AM – 9 PM) for promotional traffic.
  • Match volume to your registered campaign; sudden unregistered spikes get filtered by carriers.
  • Segment lists, remove inactive numbers, and never purchase contact lists.

7. Enforcement

We monitor deliverability and abuse signals. Violations may result in messaging suspension, campaign deregistration, or account action under the Terms of Service. Carrier fines resulting from your violations may be passed through to you.
