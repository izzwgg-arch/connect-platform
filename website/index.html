FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Loop just a VoIP provider?
No. Voice is one part of the platform. Loop is a business-software company: AI agents, office automation, delivery operations for supermarkets, contractor tools — and yes, a complete cloud phone system (Connect) that ties it all together.
Can I keep my existing phone numbers?
Yes. We port your numbers in for you and manage the whole process — you keep taking calls the entire time. New local numbers are also available instantly during signup.
What devices does the phone system work on?
iPhone and Android apps, a Windows desktop app, a browser-based phone, and standard desk phones (we provision them for you). All of them ring together and hand off cleanly.
What can the AI agent actually change?
Today it can answer questions, look up call history, change number routing, edit IVR menus and schedules, manage queues and ring groups, set Do Not Disturb, and manage hold music and announcements. Sensitive changes require owner approval, and every action is logged.
Does Loop support E911?
Yes — emergency calling is supported with registered service addresses, and VoIP 911 works differently from traditional landlines. Please read our E911 disclosure for the details that matter.
How fast can we get set up?
Our onboarding is automated end-to-end: pick a number (or port yours), add your team, and the system builds your extensions, apps and routing automatically. Most businesses are live within days.
