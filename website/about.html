About Loop

We build for the businesses
software forgot

Supermarkets running thirty deliveries a day on paper. Contractors quoting jobs from the driver's seat. Offices where the phone never stops. That's who we build for — and we build them the same grade of technology the Fortune 500 gets.

Our story

It started with a phone system

Loop began by building Connect — a cloud phone system good enough for businesses that genuinely cannot miss a call. Getting that right meant building deep: our own apps, our own monitoring, our own automation.

Then our customers kept asking for more. Track our deliveries. Chase our invoices. Change the routing without calling support. Answer in Yiddish. So we built it — all of it, on one platform, where every piece talks to every other piece.

Today Loop is an AI-agent, automation and communications company. The phone system is still the heartbeat. But the loop keeps growing.
