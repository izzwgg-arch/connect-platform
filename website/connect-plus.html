From order to doorstep

The whole delivery day,
on one screen

Live dispatch map

Every driver, every run, every stop — live. ETAs update as traffic and progress change; late or stuck orders raise exceptions before customers notice. Stale GPS pings are flagged so “live” always means live.

Route optimization

Build efficient runs automatically. Fewer miles, more on-time windows, cooler ice cream.

Order intake

Orders sync from your existing register or order system — no double entry, no new workflow for the front office.

Barcode scanning

Scan totes at load-out and at the door. The right bags, on the right truck, to the right customer.

Proof of delivery

Photos, signatures and drop-off notes captured on every stop — with configurable requirements per order type.

Customer updates

Automatic texts at dispatch, en-route and delivered — plus a live tracking link. Customers can even reply by text to reschedule.

The driver app

Drivers tap. Dispatch sees.

A driver app that survives the real world: spotty cell coverage, phones in pockets, long shifts. Location updates adapt to battery level, everything queues offline and syncs when signal returns, and navigation hands off to the maps app drivers already use.

  • ✓Run list & stop details — the day’s work, in order, with special instructions front and center.
  • ✓Offline-first — no signal, no problem; nothing gets lost.
  • ✓Battery-aware tracking — accurate location without killing the phone.
  • ✓Exception reporting — “customer not home” is one tap, and dispatch knows instantly.
