How auto-detect + Yiddish understanding works

Under the hood — the caller never sees this; they just speak and get answered in their language.
caller speaks (Yiddish OR English) │ ▼ Yiddish Labs STT language=auto ──► detects: yi / en / he / lk │ ├── English? ──► straight to the AI (Sonnet 5) ──► reply in English │ └── Yiddish? ──► translate YI→EN ──► AI reasons in English ──► translate EN→YI │ ▼ reply spoken/typed in Yiddish │ ▼ every transcript ──► corpus (tuning flywheel) + searchable call record
Two modes we'll A/B: (1) translate-bridge (ships first — reliable, uses Yiddish Labs' strength) and (2) native Yiddish LLM (later — lower latency where quality is proven). The caller experience is identical: talk, get answered.
