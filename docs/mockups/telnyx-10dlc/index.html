10DLC Registration, inside Loopcom

A new Admin page, /admin/texting-registration, where platform staff register a customer's business with the carriers through Telnyx and follow it until their number can text. One brand and one campaign per customer business (Telnyx's own reseller rule), filed from Loopcom, watched by Loopcom, and switched on in chat automatically when the carriers approve.

Nothing here is built. Businesses and numbers are examples. Dashed amber marks what is new in the sidebar. Twelve screens, then the decisions I need from you before building.

BEFORE BUILDING

Decisions I need from you

1 · Never store the EIN?

My recommendation: yes, never store it, the rule the sign-up wizard already keeps. The cost is re-typing it to fix a brand. Also found: the old 10DLC form saves EINs as plain base64 in a column called einEncrypted. I'd like to clean that up in the same build.

2 · Who pays the fees?

Charge the customer ($24 one-time on their next invoice plus the monthly fee as a recurring line), or Loopcom absorbs it. Onboarding currently tells customers $15 one-time with the monthly fee included.

3 · Platform staff only?

My recommendation: staff only (like Carrier Migration and the Telnyx page), with the four keys grantable to staff roles only. Customers don't see this page.

4 · When a number is approved, move its texting to Telnyx automatically?

My recommendation: yes, reusing the Phase 1 messaging router, with the previous carrier kept as the backup route. The alternative is an "Approved, switch when ready" button per number.

5 · Sole-proprietor PIN: staff enters it, or a link for the owner?

My recommendation: both, with the link as the default so staff never handle it.

6 · Also fix the SMS Campaigns page gate?

Found while researching: its 10DLC check reads a response shape the API doesn't send, so it always says "not registered", even for an approved customer. Fixing it means pointing it at this system's status.

Build order once approved: schema (additive), Telnyx 10DLC client with a test for every call, filing and re-check engine, signed status webhook, the page and its toggles, then a live filing on Loopcom's own business as proof before any customer is filed.
