10DLC Registration, v3: the customer fills it in

Each customer gets a private link. You copy it or send it in a standard Loopcom email. The customer opens a Loopcom-branded form in light or dark mode where everything the system already knows or can write is shown but locked, and only what we genuinely need from them can be typed. Their answers land in Loopcom. You review them and press File with Telnyx. Filing automatically can be switched on later.

Nothing here is built. Businesses, people and numbers are examples. The customer's email and form never mention a carrier by name.

You: send link→Customer: fills in 7 things→You: review, File with Telnyx→Carriers review→Texting turns on

BEFORE BUILDING

What changed from v1, and what I still need

Settled

Customer link plus email; customers see the system-written parts but can't edit them; you press File with Telnyx; automatic filing later. EIN is tokenized: stored as an encrypted token, shown as the last 4 digits, revealed only with a permission and a history entry, destroyed once the registry verifies the business (or after 14 days if never filed). The old form's base64 EINs get the same treatment. The $24.00 up-front charge goes on the customer's invoice. The monthly fee and marketing texts are left out entirely until you decide; nothing on any screen mentions them.

1 · Privacy policy: researched, here's the plan

The registry doesn't technically require it (the field is optional at The Campaign Registry and at Telnyx), but its own guide says a compliant policy is required, and reviewers reject without one. That holds even when customers text first: that only removes the need for extra permission, not the policy. Telnyx says the policy must be the business's own, never the reseller's. It does not say where it has to live. Other providers accept a public PDF, a Google Drive link, or a Google Business or Facebook page as the business's online presence. Plan: Loopcom writes a short privacy policy and SMS terms in each customer's legal name, publishes them at a public page (loopcom.net/sms/hudson-valley-tire), and attaches them to the filing. Customers without a website give their Google Business or Facebook link instead. Still unproven until the first real filings: whether carriers accept a policy in the customer's name hosted on Loopcom's domain. OK to go with this?

2 · When approved, move the numbers' texting to Telnyx automatically?

My recommendation: yes, keeping the old carrier as backup.

3 · Fix the two old 10DLC checks too?

The SMS Campaigns page check that can never pass, and the live-texting switch that only reads the old form's table.
