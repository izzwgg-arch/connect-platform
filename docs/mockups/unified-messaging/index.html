5 · Honest delivery states, fallback and failure — all in today's meta line

The meta line already says time · sent ✓. New states are more words in that same 11px row: never a redesigned bubble, never a faked tick. VoIP.ms numbers cap at “sent” because VoIP.ms sends no delivery receipts; Telnyx/SignalWire earn “delivered”; RCS/WhatsApp/Messenger earn “read”.
