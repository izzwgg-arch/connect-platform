Why it looks like this
Today, creating a line means typing a company's internal ID into a box at the bottom of the fleet table, then finding a separate button on the new row to buy its eSIM. This puts the whole job on one page, in the order you think about it: whose line → who carries it → which plan → which SIM → which number. The right column always shows both sides of the money — what the customer is billed and what LoopCom pays the carrier — and the live carrier balance, so a purchase that would fail for low balance is caught before you press anything. The SIM step offers stock first, so eSIMs already bought and SIM cards already on the shelf get used before buying new.

Add a line is the page's main button.
