DNC Dialing Gate

Four screens. The rule you asked for: turning on CRM for a tenant arms the gate by default — their CRM extensions can't place calls until Do-Not-Call setup is complete — and you get a per-tenant switch in the admin panel to turn it off for the ones that aren't cold calling.
