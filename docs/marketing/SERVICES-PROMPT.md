```
Write the website for Loopcom.

COMPANY
Loopcom is the AI communications platform for business. One system for
everything a business says to a customer — every call, every message, every
voicemail, and the customer record underneath it all.

Positioning line: "We build everything and anything phone-related."

What makes us different: most business phone providers resell someone else's
platform. We run our own — the switching, the routing, the call handling, all
of it. So when a customer needs something specific, we can actually build it
instead of explaining why the vendor won't. And because the phone system, the
customer records and the messaging sit on one database, nothing has to be
synced, searched for, or asked twice.

Audience: B2B. Small and mid-size business owners losing money to calls nobody
answered and voicemails nobody checked. Write to an owner, not to procurement.

SERVICES

Phone system
- Softphone in the browser, on mobile, and on desktop — no handsets needed,
  though desk phones work too
- Auto-attendant / IVR menus with your own recorded prompts
- Routing that changes automatically for business hours, after hours and
  holidays
- One-click override to close early or for emergencies
- Call queues and ring groups
- Direct number (DID) routing to any destination
- Hold, transfer, and multi-call handling
- Call recording and playback
- Voicemail with greetings you upload or record from your phone
- Music on hold, with scheduling
- Announcements and system recordings
- Missed-call alerts and push notifications
- Extension provisioning with QR-code device pairing

Customers / CRM
- Contacts with tags, notes and full history
- One timeline per customer covering every call, text, email and voicemail
- Campaigns and calling queues for your team
- Email suite — templates, branding, signatures, bulk sending with scheduling
- Forms your customers fill in from a link
- Call scripts and checklists
- Tasks and notes
- Bulk import
- Voicemail drops — leave a prerecorded message without ringing
- Local-presence caller ID
- Live-call screen-pop — the customer record opens before you pick up

Messaging
- SMS and MMS from your business number, fully threaded
- Shared team inbox or personal inbox, per number
- Message templates
- Bulk campaigns with approval before anything sends
- Carrier (10DLC) registration handled for you
- Internal team chat with reactions, read receipts and file sharing

AI
- Call and voicemail transcription in English and Yiddish — transcribed in the
  language it was spoken and summarised in English, so the record is
  searchable and readable by whoever picks it up next
- Call summaries with action items pulled out
- Sentiment and intent detection
- Lead research reports before you call a new prospect
- AI email writing and rewriting
- An assistant that answers and acts across calls, messages and voicemail

Analytics
- Live monitoring of every call in progress
- Full call history and detailed records
- Call quality reporting
- Queue performance
- Dashboard KPIs and call traffic
- IVR analytics — how people move through your menus
- CSV export

Billing (for businesses that invoice their own customers)
- Branded invoices as PDFs
- Automatic payment collection with retries
- Usage metering
- Tax and telecom fees worked out
- A hosted payment page your customers pay from without ringing anyone

Apps and administration
- Web portal, Android app, and desktop app for Windows and Mac
- Calls ring even when the window is closed
- User invites, roles and custom permission sets
- Phone number management and porting
- Guided onboarding — we set it up with you and build your first menu together

PRICING
$30 per user, per month. Tax included.
First phone number included. No setup fee, no contract, no feature tiers —
every account gets the whole platform. Additional phone numbers and messaging
are billed per use.

CONTACT
WhatsApp +1 845 733 1213
loopcom.com

VOICE
Direct and concrete. Short sentences. Say the thing, don't tease it. Write
features as what they do for the owner, not as spec-sheet nouns — "routing
that changes automatically for evenings and holidays", not "time-based
conditions". No "revolutionize", "seamless", "unlock", "empower", "solutions",
"synergy", "game-changing". No exclamation marks.

VISUAL
Dark. Near-black ground (#0C1218 to #000206), never white or light grey.
One accent family: cyan #22A8FF into indigo #4F7BFF. Highlights #7FE7FF.
Never violet, purple, magenta, lavender, pastel gradients, organic blob shapes
or frosted glassmorphic pills — that is our nearest competitor's territory.
Hard edges with 45-degree chamfered corners, no rounded bubbles.
Type: Chakra Petch for headings, IBM Plex Sans for body, IBM Plex Mono for
small uppercase labels with wide letterspacing.
Mood: nocturnal, engineered, precise, calm. Infrastructure that works.

DO NOT MENTION
Sign-in or account creation — this is a presence site, not an app.
Delivery or order tracking.
```
